Rules of KermitKrack

By Kralled

Rules

No killing people (or their pets/animals) in aggression or if it would result in the loss of their items. Some messing around should be okay, but please have some decency about this and don't be mean.


No griefing, modifying or damaging people's property without their permission. People's property means their structures and items, in both claimed and unclaimed land. Like above, a small bit of messing around is okay, but please use some common sense.


Modifications to your client which would give yourself an unfair advantage and/or act to undermind survival gameplay are prohibited. This includes, x-ray, hacks, or using websites such as Chunkbase. Please don't exploit glitches in the game, particularly ones that duplicate items or entities. TNT dupers and bedrock breaking bugs are exempt from this. If unsure about whether a mod would be allowed or not, ask. Here are some example modifications to reduce ambiguity:

- Xaeros: Allowed for most purposes, but using it to see structures underground when on the surface is a bit cheesy, so don't do that.

- Full bright: I don't like it much, but for now it is allowed.


Land can be claimed by requesting a claim to 'Kralled' who will either approve it or deny it. A map with all the current land claims will be published under '⁠land-claims' . People have full ownership of the land inside their claim, and have "implied rights" over the land surrounding it. This means you can't go destroying the land next to someone's base or building in it, and that someone does have greater priority in claiming the land next to their claim, then someone who doesn't border it would. If you build someone in unclaimed land, you generally own the land surrounding. Land claims mean someone owns land from the bottom of the world to the top, whilst unclaimed land typically means surface to the top and if underground, then just the area surrounding it.


Be civil. No excessive shouting and swearing and especially no threatening behaviour.


Don't leak the IP address of the server to non-members or intentionally lag the server, please have some respect for your poor server owner :)

The following rules are also placed here which may change at anytime
